      Under the Hood

      Behind the Analysis

      Breaks text into smaller pieces called tokens so the application can analyze the content.

      Filters out common words like "the" or "and" that carry little meaning, so the analysis focuses on meaningful terms.

      Identifies the most frequent, meaningful terms in a document to summarize what it emphasizes.

      Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency weighs each word by how often it appears in a document versus how common it is overall, highlighting distinctive terms.

      Measures the angle between two documents' term vectors to score how similar their language is, from 0% to 100%.

      Compares the recognized skills in the resume against those required by the job description to find matches and gaps.

      Private by design. Your documents are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to a server.
